# Cat Ina Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A third form of Ina, cat Ina (Ninyomanyo Ninya'nis), rolled on about 1 wake in 5: surprised arrival, ears and a heart circle, nyanified lines, eared takodachis, Ao-chan with ears and a tail, a cat trophy and a love version of Ao-chan's show, and "BYE NYA~" back to regular Ina.

**Architecture:** `Scene.form` gains `'cat'` and `Tako` gains `ears`. Everything of hers lives in a new `cat.js` (art, lines, `nyan()`, CSS). `scene.js` picks it by form, as it does `frieren.js`, and emits her art, CSS and defs **only** when she (or an eared takodachi) is on screen. The shows (`special.js`) take a `cat` flag and swap in her variants; there are no new show kinds.

**Tech Stack:** Plain ES modules producing SVG strings. Tests are in `plugins/anime-themes/hooks/register.test.tsx`, run with `claude plugin test plugins/anime-themes`. Flash check: `node tools/flash-check/check.mjs --control --sizes all`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-18-cat-ina-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Roll, in `wake` from sleep: Frieren as now (`roll % 4 === 0`, `roll = hash('frieren:' + id)`). Otherwise cat when `(roll >>> 14) % 15 < 4`, which gives 66 of the 400 test ids. With this slice none of `t`, `t1`, `t2`, `t3`, `next` rolls cat. The Ao-chan and hair rolls are unchanged.
- Lines, verbatim:
  - wake `INYA?!?` (at 0.4 s);
  - Ao-chan greeting `AO-NYAN!!` (at 2.0 s, `dur` 1.8);
  - farewell `BYE NYA~` (every ending; with `ask`, the standing ask line follows as for Frieren);
  - her pool: `IT IS I,\nNINYOMANYO\nNINYA'NIS!`, `INYA, INYA, INYA`, `TAKONYACHI!`, `NYA-T IS THIS?`, `PURR PURR~`, `PAWS FOR A BREAK~`, `HISSS... A BUG!`, `KNEADING THE CODE~`, `LOVE THE FLOOR,\nINYA~`;
  - show lines: trophy `ANOTHER\nGOLD TROPHY\nFOR NINYOMANYO\nNINYA'NIS!`, Ao-chan `WORLD\nDOM{INYA}TION`.
- `nyan(text)`: `AO-CHAN`→`AO-NYAN`, then `WAH`→`NYAH`, `NA`→`NYA`, `NO`→`NYO`. It applies to shared lines only (`SAY.*`, `AO_SAY`, the looping `WAH!`), and only while `form === 'cat'`.
- Timings:
  - Arrival: eyes open at 0.4 s, sparkles 1.0 s, left ear 1.3 s, right ear 1.7 s, sigil→heart over 1.3–2.1 s, `:3` face from 2.2 s; Ao-chan rises at `--a` 1.9 s.
  - Exit, after `--h`: ears down 1.8 s (right) then 2.1 s (left), heart→sigil over 2.0–2.8 s, regular Ina at 2.8 s.
  - Lull `--d`: 4 s plus hold. A prompt show waits 4 s for her.
- Flash guard: big swaps no faster than about 0.8 s a frame, steps not smooth on moving sprites, single hops, one faint window flash per show.
- Size:
  - Every SVG stays under 131,072 characters.
  - Frieren's worst case (127,719 today) must not grow: nothing of hers goes in the shared `CSS` or `DEFS`.
  - The heart circle is at most about 9,000 characters (the round sigil is about 10,800); the rest of hers in her worst case is at most about 15,000.
- Atom shape tag `scene-6` → `scene-7`.
- Resting state is static style: with reduced motion she shows ears, `:3` face and heart circle in `work` and `wait`; nothing of hers animates in `wait`.

## Review Focus

1. A redraw mid-arrival (`join(wake(cat))`): she's drawn at rest, ears up, heart circle, no pop replayed. Test in Task 3.
2. A question mid-turn (`ask(cat)`): ears, face and heart circle visible, and none of her parts animate. Test in Task 3.
3. An eared takodachi still out when the next turn rolls Frieren or nerd Ina: it keeps its ears, and the `#cear` def is emitted. Test in Task 4.
4. A turn ending mid-arrival or mid-show: the exit plays from rest, and "BYE NYA~" waits for the show. Test in Task 2; flash-check case in Task 6.
5. Reduced motion: she must still be visibly cat Ina in `work`. Checked by eye in Task 6.
